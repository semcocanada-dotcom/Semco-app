Autism Fund Tracker Support

Help with accounts, receipts, mileage, worksheets, and privacy
Contact support

Email info@semcocanada.ca or call +1 (306) 530-7910 with a description of the issue. Do not email passwords, health card numbers, receipts, or other sensitive family information.

Account access

If you cannot access your account, contact support from the email address associated with the account. Support will never ask you to send your password.

Delete your account

In the app, open Profile, choose Delete Account, and complete the confirmation. This permanently deletes the account and its associated profile, children, funding years, expenses, mileage, respite records, appointments, claims, custom providers, and receipt files. The deletion cannot be undone.

Receipts and worksheets

Receipt text recognition is optional. After you choose a receipt, the app shows a disclosure before anything is sent to Google Cloud Vision. Choose Attach without recognition to keep the receipt and enter expense details manually, or choose Use text recognition to send that receipt for recognition. Generated worksheets can be saved or shared from the app.

Find a service provider

Autism Fund Tracker does not reproduce or endorse a public provider directory. In the app, open Providers and choose Open Official Saskatchewan Registry to view the Government of Saskatchewan Registry of Autism Service Providers in your browser. Providers you add in the app are private records visible only to your account; they are not verified against or represented as listed on the official registry.

Mileage

Enter trip distance and the applicable reimbursement rate manually. The app does not request device location or send addresses or coordinates to a mapping service.

Funding amount estimates

Any age-based amount shown by the app is an editable estimate based on Saskatchewan program information current as of August 2026. It is not an eligibility or approval decision. Confirm the child’s actual approval and amount with Saskatchewan, then enter the approved amount for the funding year. See the official Saskatchewan autism services page for current program information.

Consent for child and respite-worker information

An adult parent, legal guardian, or otherwise authorized person must accept the child-data notice before first saving a child. Before saving respite-worker or session details, the account holder must confirm they have that person’s permission or other legal authority. The consent boxes start unchecked. Choosing Cancel or closing the form does not save those details. Read the Privacy Policy below for the fields and purposes involved.

Worksheets and government submissions

Every exported PDF is an unbranded Autism Fund Tracker worksheet marked “Unofficial — not a Government of Saskatchewan form” and states that Autism Fund Tracker is independent and not affiliated with or endorsed by the Government of Saskatchewan. A worksheet is not a government form, claim, application, approval, or official submission, and the app does not submit it. The app opens the official Saskatchewan ASD-IF expense portal when you are ready to complete an official submission.

Use these one-tap links for the untouched government PDFs:

These links open files hosted on publications.saskatchewan.ca. Autism Fund Tracker does not embed, alter, or reproduce those government PDFs or their artwork.
