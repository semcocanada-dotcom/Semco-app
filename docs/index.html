Privacy Policy

Autism Fund Tracker · Last updated 7 August 2026

Autism Fund Tracker (“the app”) is operated by Innovative Finishes Inc. (“we,” “us”). The app helps Saskatchewan families track their child’s Autism Spectrum Disorder Individualized Funding (ASD-IF) grant. We take the privacy of your family’s information seriously, especially because the app handles children’s personal and health-related information. This policy explains what we collect, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account information — the name and email address you provide when creating an account.
  • Child profile information — the child’s name, date of birth, and (optionally) their Saskatchewan Health Services Number, diagnosis date, and diagnosis notes or other health details you choose to enter. We use this information to keep the optional health context alongside the child’s private funding records.
  • Funding and expense records — expenses you log, the providers you visit, receipt images or PDFs you upload, mileage trips, and appointment details. A receipt image may show payment-method information printed by the merchant, such as a card type or masked digits.
  • Private/custom provider information — a provider or organization name, service category or relationship to your family, street address, city, postal code, and notes that you choose to enter. These entries are private to your account and help you associate your own records with expenses, mileage, and appointments.
  • Respite-worker information — a worker’s name, phone number, default rate, relationship or role details you include in notes, and respite-session notes. We use it to let you select a worker again, fill your respite records and worksheets, and calculate recorded respite amounts.
Health and Sensitive Information: A child’s diagnosis date, diagnosis notes, Health Services Number, and information about their providers or respite workers may reveal health, disability, care, or family relationship details. We treat this data as Health and Sensitive Info and use it only to provide the recordkeeping features described in this policy.

How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account and let you sign in.
  • To track each child’s grant balance, expenses, mileage, and appointments.
  • To read receipt details automatically (text recognition) so logging an expense is faster.
  • To check for and deliver app updates and diagnose update failures.
  • To create unbranded Autism Fund Tracker expense, mileage, respite, and grant-summary worksheets for personal recordkeeping.

Every exported worksheet is labeled “Unofficial — not a Government of Saskatchewan form” and states that Autism Fund Tracker is independent and not affiliated with or endorsed by the Government of Saskatchewan. Worksheets are for personal recordkeeping only; they are not government forms, applications, approvals, or official submissions, and the app does not send them automatically.

The app also provides external links to the untouched official mileage form, respite form, and online expense submission portal. Those government resources open from Saskatchewan-hosted websites and are not embedded, modified, or reproduced by the app.

Location and mileage

The app does not request or use your device’s location and does not send addresses or coordinates to a mapping or geocoding service. Mileage distance is entered manually, along with the applicable rate, when recording a trip.

Saskatchewan provider registry

The app does not copy, store, rank, verify, or endorse the public Saskatchewan Registry of Autism Service Providers. Choosing Open Official Saskatchewan Registry opens the Government of Saskatchewan webpage in your device browser. Any provider entries you add inside the app are your private records and are not represented as approved or registry-listed.

How your data is stored and protected

Your data is stored in an encrypted database hosted by Supabase in the Canada Central region. Each account can only access its own data, enforced by row-level security. Receipt files are kept in a private storage bucket and are only ever accessed through short-lived, signed links. Your login session is stored on your device using the operating system’s secure keychain.

Third-party services

We rely on a small number of trusted services to run the app:

  • Supabase — account authentication, database hosting, and private receipt storage in Canada Central.
  • Google Cloud Vision — receives a chosen receipt image or PDF only after the app shows a disclosure and you select Use text recognition. You can instead attach the receipt without recognition and enter expense details manually.
  • Expo Application Services — checks for and delivers app updates. Expo may process the device operating system, a randomized persistent installation token, update checks or downloads, and limited crash, error, or failed-update information. These technical events may be associated with the device installation but are not attached to the app account. They are used only for app functionality, update analytics, and diagnostics, not advertising or tracking.

We require service providers handling app data to protect it consistently with this policy and applicable privacy requirements. We do not sell personal information, use it for advertising, or permit tracking.

Children’s information

The app is intended for an adult parent, legal guardian, or another person who is authorized to manage the child’s information. Before the first child record is saved, the account holder must affirm that authority, acknowledge that the Health Services Number and diagnosis information are optional, and agree to this Privacy Policy. The app stores the notice version and acceptance time with the child record so the authorization can be documented. A child record is only accessible from the account that created it.

Information about respite workers and other people

Before saving a respite worker or a respite session, the account holder must affirm that they have the person’s permission, or other legal authority, to store the person’s name, phone number, rate, relationship details, and notes. The app stores the notice version and acceptance time with the worker or session record. Do not enter another person’s information if you do not have that permission or authority.

Data retention and deletion

Your information is kept for as long as you maintain an account. To permanently delete your account and its associated profile, child, funding, expense, mileage, respite, appointment, provider, and receipt data, open Profile in the app, choose Delete Account, and complete the confirmation. Deletion is processed immediately and cannot be undone. Contact us if you cannot access the in-app option.

Your choices

  • Edit or delete expenses, trips, providers, and child profiles at any time within the app.
  • Calendar export and notification reminders are off by default. The app asks for each permission only after you turn on that option for an appointment. Notification text is generic on the lock screen.
  • Control camera, calendar, and notification permissions in your device settings.
  • Permanently delete your account and associated data from Profile > Delete Account.
  • Request a copy of your data or ask a privacy question by emailing us.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app.
