Privacy Policy

Autism Funding Tracker · Last updated 7 June 2026

Autism Funding Tracker (“the app,” “we,” “us”) helps Saskatchewan families track their child’s Autism Spectrum Disorder Individualized Funding (ASD-IF) grant. We take the privacy of your family’s information seriously, especially because the app handles children’s personal and health-related information. This policy explains what we collect, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account information — the email address you sign up with. If you choose to sign in with Apple or Google, we receive the name and email address those services share with us to create your account.
  • Child profile information — the child’s name, date of birth, and (optionally) their Saskatchewan Health Services Number, which you enter to manage their grant.
  • Funding and expense records — expenses you log, the providers you visit, receipt images or PDFs you upload, mileage trips, and appointment details.
  • Home address — used only to calculate driving distance for mileage reimbursement claims.

How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account and let you sign in.
  • To track each child’s grant balance, expenses, mileage, and appointments.
  • To read receipt details automatically (text recognition) so logging an expense is faster.
  • To pre-fill the official Saskatchewan mileage and respite reimbursement forms you export.

Location

With your permission, the app uses your device’s location to show nearby approved providers and to help auto-fill mileage distances. Your location is used on the device for these calculations and is not stored on our servers or shared. You can deny or revoke location access at any time in your device settings; the rest of the app continues to work.

How your data is stored and protected

Your data is stored in an encrypted database hosted by Supabase in the Canada Central region. Each account can only access its own data, enforced by row-level security. Receipt files are kept in a private storage bucket and are only ever accessed through short-lived, signed links. Your login session is stored on your device using the operating system’s secure keychain.

Third-party services

We rely on a small number of trusted services to run the app:

  • Supabase — secure database, authentication, and file storage.
  • Google Cloud Vision — reads text from receipt images you upload.
  • Google Maps Platform — geocoding and driving-distance calculations for mileage.
  • Apple & Google Sign-In — optional sign-in methods, if you choose to use them.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Children’s information

The app is used by parents and guardians to manage funding on behalf of their children. Information about a child is entered by you, the parent or guardian, and is only accessible from your account.

Data retention and deletion

Your information is kept for as long as you maintain an account. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us at the email below, and we will permanently remove it.

Your choices

  • Edit or delete expenses, trips, providers, and child profiles at any time within the app.
  • Control camera, photo, location, calendar, and notification permissions in your device settings.
  • Request a copy of, or deletion of, your data by emailing us.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app.
